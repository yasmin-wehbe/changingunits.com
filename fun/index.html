About fun unit conversions

Fun units are informal measurements used to make large or abstract distances more relatable. From the smoot (invented at MIT in 1958) to bananas (used in science communication), these quirky units help put scale into perspective.

The smoot is perhaps the most famous novelty unit — Oliver Smoot was laid end-to-end 364.4 times to measure the Harvard Bridge in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Google Earth and Google Maps even supported smoot measurements for a time.

Pop culture has added its own units: lightsabers, the Millennium Falcon, and the Death Star all have official documented dimensions from Lucasfilm and can be used as genuine length references.
