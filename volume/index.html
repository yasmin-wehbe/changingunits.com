About volume conversions

Volume conversions are essential for cooking, chemistry, fuel measurement, and liquid handling. The most common conversions are between liters (metric) and gallons (US/imperial), and between milliliters and fluid ounces for smaller quantities.

Note that US and imperial (UK) gallons differ significantly — one US gallon equals 3.785 liters while one imperial gallon equals 4.546 liters. Always check which standard applies when doing international conversions.

For the international definition, see the NIST reference on SI units.
