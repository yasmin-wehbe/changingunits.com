About length conversions

Length is one of the most commonly converted measurements, particularly between the metric system (millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers) used in most of the world, and the imperial and US customary systems (inches, feet, yards, miles) used primarily in the United States and, for some measurements, the United Kingdom.

The metric system is based on powers of ten, making conversions within the system straightforward. The imperial system uses historical units — one inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters, one foot equals 12 inches, and one mile equals 5,280 feet.

Common uses include converting height between feet and centimeters, driving distances between kilometers and miles, and reading product dimensions when shopping internationally. For the international definition of length units, see the NIST reference on SI units of length.
