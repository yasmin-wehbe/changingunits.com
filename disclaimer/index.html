Conversion factors

We use exact or internationally recognised values wherever they exist. That said, some units have definitions that vary by country, industry, or historical era — a fluid ounce in the US is not the same as one in the UK, and a ton in the US is not the same as a tonne. We label which standard we're using, but if your use case depends on a specific regional or industry definition, double-check which one applies.

If you spot a wrong conversion factor, please report it and we'll fix it.
